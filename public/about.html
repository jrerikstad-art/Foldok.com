About, Mission & Vision

About us

Foldok builds tools for people who must produce engineering documentation — manuals, compliance packages, as-builts, inspection records — from real project material.

We started from a simple observation: the work on site and in the folder is already done; the package is still assembled late, by hand, across photos, PDFs, drawings, and memory. Gaps show up at handover. Traceability is weak. Generic AI tools help you draft; they do not hold the project together.

Foldok is local-first. Your files stay in your folders. The product helps you see what is missing, close it with evidence, and deliver a package you can stand behind. AI is used only where it helps — gated, inspectable, never a substitute for your judgment.

We are building an engineering documentation operating system: capture in the field, knowledge and gaps on the laptop, structured documents out — so finishing the package is easier than leaving it incomplete.

Mission

Make incomplete engineering documentation hard to ignore — and complete packages the path of least resistance.

Vision

Foldok becomes the place engineering documentation lives — from first site photo to delivered package and later updates.

We help engineers and teams:

  • work from the documents and evidence they already have
  • surface what is missing before handover
  • build, edit, and close gaps with traceable sources
  • deliver packages with clarity, not hope

We do not exist to help anyone “get away with” thin paperwork. We exist so the missing pieces are visible, and fixing them is the natural next step.

In that future

  • The project folder is the source of truth; Foldok organises, links, and completes around it
  • Field capture feeds the same project the office compiles
  • Requirements and evidence stay bound to sections and deliverables
  • AI stays infrastructure — countable, masked when needed, never the author of record
  • Teams of one or of many use the same lifecycle; responsibilities stay optional, not forced process theatre

We aim for documentation that behaves more like engineered work: structured, traceable, reproducible under change — so when the job moves on, the package does not fall apart.

How we work

  1. Documentation is the workspace Projects give context; the package is the job.
  2. Truth before polish Gaps and sources first; layout second.
  3. Local-first Your files, your standards licences, your machine.
  4. Fix over ignore Every feature should make closing a gap easier than skipping it.
  5. You remain responsible The tool assists; you sign and ship.

What “compliance” means here

Foldok does not know the law the way a lawyer or a standards body does. It knows what a requirement profile said must exist, then checks whether evidence is present — artifact linked, provenance confirmed. “Ready” means ready relative to this pack, not “legally compliant” or “certified to NEK 400.” You (or your competent person) still own the meaning.
